# Stock Insights — Your Personal Stock Market Guide

This app was built for you to track Indian stocks, get honest analysis, and stay informed — without needing to understand any technical terms.

Open the app here: **https://stock-market-three-nu.vercel.app**

---

## Pages at a Glance

### 1. Dashboard
This is the home page. Every time you open the app, start here.

**What you'll see:**
- **NIFTY 50, SENSEX, NIFTY Bank, NIFTY IT** — the main Indian market indices and how they're doing today
- **AI Market Overview** — a short plain-English summary of today's market mood
- **Top Gainers** — stocks that went up the most today
- **Top Losers** — stocks that fell the most today

**When to use it:** Every morning before the market opens, or during the day to check how things are going.

---

### 2. Stock Search
Search for any stock and see its full price history as a chart.

**How to use:**
1. Type a company name (e.g. "Reliance" or "Infosys") in the search bar
2. Select from the suggestions that appear
3. The chart will show you how the stock has moved over time

**What the chart shows:** The closing price of the stock for the past year, so you can see if it has been going up or down overall.

---

### 3. AI Insights
This is the most useful page. Type any company name and get an honest, plain-English analysis of that stock.

**How to use:**
1. Type a company name (e.g. "TCS", "HDFC Bank", "Wipro")
2. Select from the dropdown
3. Click **Get Insight**
4. Wait 10–20 seconds for the AI to analyse the stock

**What you'll get:**
- A direct verdict — *"Looks good right now"* or *"Stay away for now"* — with a clear reason
- What's working in its favour
- What risks to watch out for
- Simple advice on what to keep in mind

**Important:** This is for information only, not financial advice. Always use your own judgement before buying or selling.

---

### 4. Predictions
This page uses a mathematical model (not just guesswork) to estimate where a stock's price might go.

**How to use:**
1. Search for a stock by company name
2. Click **Predict**
3. Wait about 60 seconds — this is normal, the model is doing heavy calculations
4. You'll see price estimates for **1 Week, 1 Month, 3 Months, and 6 Months**

**What the numbers mean:**
- **Predicted Price** — where the model thinks the price could go
- **Confidence Range** — the range within which it is likely to land (e.g. ₹1,100 – ₹1,200)
- **Current Price** is shown at the top so you can compare directly

**Important:** These predictions are based only on past price patterns. They do not account for news, company results, or world events. Treat them as one input, not a guarantee.

---

### 5. Trending
Shows which stocks are getting unusually high attention today — based on trading volume and price movement.

**What it means:** If a stock is trending, something is happening with it — good or bad. The AI explains in simple words what the pattern might mean.

**When to use it:** When you want to know what the market is excited or worried about today.

---

### 6. Watchlist
Your personal list of stocks you want to keep an eye on.

**How to use:**
1. Search for a company name and select it — it gets added immediately
2. You'll see the current price, today's change, and performance over 5, 20, and 60 days
3. Click **Set Alert** on any stock to be notified by email if the price crosses a level you set

**Setting a price alert:**
- **Alert above ₹___** — you'll get an email if the stock rises above this price
- **Alert below ₹___** — you'll get an email if the stock falls below this price
- Click Save. That's it.

**To save your email for alerts:**
- Enter your email address in the box at the top of the Watchlist page and click Save
- You only need to do this once

---

### 7. Ask AI
Ask anything about stocks in plain English — like texting a knowledgeable friend.

**Examples of what you can ask:**
- *"Should I sell my Infosys shares? I bought them at ₹1,400"*
- *"Is TCS a good buy right now?"*
- *"What is happening with the pharma sector?"*
- *"Compare Reliance and HDFC Bank"*
- *"My WIPRO is down 10%, should I be worried?"*

**How it works:** The AI looks up the current price and technical data for the stock you mention, then gives you a direct recommendation with a clear reason.

**The AI will:**
- Give you a recommendation first, then explain why
- Speak plainly — no jargon
- Be honest even if the answer is "avoid this stock right now"

---

## Daily Emails

Once your email is saved in the Watchlist page, you will receive two emails every weekday:

| Email | Time | What's inside |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning Report** | 9:15 AM IST | Market indices, top movers, your watchlist stocks |
| **Closing Report** | 3:35 PM IST | How the day ended, your watchlist final prices |

You will also get an instant email whenever a stock in your watchlist crosses a price alert you set.

---

## Tips

- **App is slow to load?** Wait 30–60 seconds on the first open. It wakes up automatically — just be patient once.
- **Predictions take long?** This is normal. The model needs about 60 seconds to calculate. Don't close the page.
- **Not sure which stock to search?** Just type the company name in plain English — "Tata Motors", "State Bank", "Bajaj Finance" — and the dropdown will find it.
- **Your watchlist is saved** — you don't need to re-add stocks every time.
